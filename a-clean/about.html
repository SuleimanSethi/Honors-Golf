Our story

Honors started in [year] in [City, State], when [founder’s name] set out to give clubs golf goods worth handing out: not giveaways that end up in a drawer, but the hats, balls and polos golfers actually reach for.

For most clubs, custom gear meant emails, mockups and weeks of waiting on a proof. We thought ordering a hundred hats with your crest on them should be as simple as buying one. So we built Honors around it. You design it on the site, see it on the product and pay in one sitting, and we make it and ship it.

Today we supply pro shops, member-guests, charity days and company golf days with rope hats, tour golf balls, apparel and accessories. Every piece is made to your design, and every design keeps its number, so next season’s order takes a minute.

How we work

Goods worth keeping

We pick pieces golfers actually use: perforated rope hats, tour golf balls, quick-dry polos. A tee gift should end up in the bag, not the drawer.

Priced for volume

Every product has price breaks. The more of one design you order, the less each piece costs, and your cart works it out as you go.

See it before you pay

You design on the product itself, so what you approve is what we make. No quote requests, and no chasing proofs by email.

Reorder in a minute

Every design keeps its own number. When the pro shop runs low, order the same hat again without starting over.
