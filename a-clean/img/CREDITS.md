# Image credits

Stand-in photos for the prototype home page (D-51), until Chuck has his own photography. All are public domain (CC0), found through Openverse. No attribution is required, but it is kept here. Hat images (`hat-*.webp`) are our own renders of the rope hat model (D-37), made with `../../tools/render_hat.html`. The tumbler, towel, divot tool, ball marker and tee images are our own renders from `../../tools/render_goods.html` (D-53). The blank tumbler views for the accessory page (UI-12), `tumbler-{forest,white,black,steel}-{front,back}.webp`, are renders from the same tool at `zoom=1&size=1600` (color inputs `#3A5243`, `#EFEDE8`, `#303030` and `steel`): the back is `yaw=180`, which turns the Honors mark away from the camera; the front is that body with the lid from `yaw=0`, so the lid's opening faces the camera; the shadow is faded out before the right edge.

The polo views for the apparel page (UI-13), `polo-{white,navy,forest,black,grey}-{front,back}.webp`, are one CC0 render of a blank-style polo, front and back of the same garment ("EEPISAT Black Polo Shirt" by Mukhayyar, Wikimedia Commons, own work, CC0 1.0; checked on the file's Commons record). Its printed team names and logos were removed (each print filled with the surrounding shading plus knit texture taken from clean fabric nearby), and a contrast-stretched check shows no watermark or trace of the prints. Each color is the same render recolored with luminance-preserving tinting: every garment pixel keeps its brightness ratio to the fabric's lit tone in linear light, times the color (White uses a softer ratio with highlights that roll off rather than clip). Each view is cropped to 1600 px square with a transparent background; the floor shadow is kept as a soft transparent black, faded out before the edges.
