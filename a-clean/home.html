Built for the people who run golf events.

Honors supplies tournaments, outings and the companies behind them with the things golfers actually keep: hats, golf balls, tumblers, towels and polos. Everything carries your logo, and everything is priced for ordering by the field, not one at a time.

Custom golf gear used to mean emails, mockups and weeks of waiting for a proof. Honors puts the whole order in your hands. Design it, see it on the product, and pay in one sitting, or ask for a quote. We make it and ship it.

Common questions

Something else? Contact us

What are the minimums?

Minimums are per design: 6 dozen for golf balls and 24 for hats. Every other product shows its minimum on its own page. [Example numbers until Chuck confirms them.]

How does volume pricing work?

Each product has price breaks. The price per piece drops as a design passes each break, and your cart applies the right price on its own.

Can I get a quote instead of checking out?

Yes, for any order. Use Request a quote on a product or in your cart, and we reply with an invoice you can pay online. Polos are always quoted, since we order them from the brand for each event.

Can I choose when it arrives?

Yes. Pick the date you need your order by, at least a week from the day you order. We confirm the date, and if it’s too tight we get in touch before we start.

What artwork can I upload?

PNG, JPG, SVG or PDF, up to 25 MB. We remove a plain white background automatically, and you can undo it.

Will the product look like the preview?

Close to it. The preview shows your artwork on the product, but it’s an approximation, especially for stitched and woven patches.

How long does production take?

[Production times from Chuck.] Once you order, you can follow each order’s stage in your account.

Do I need an account?

No. You can design and check out as a guest. An account keeps your designs and logos in one place and makes reordering quick.
